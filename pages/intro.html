Intro

欢迎访问蒋凌翔课题组！

我们是一个充满活力、温暖友爱的科研大家庭，致力于融合化学、物理、生物与光学的交叉学科前沿，开展富有创造力的高水平研究。课题组秉持开放、协作、创新的科研理念，为成员提供卓越的学术平台和成长环境。

Welcome to the Jiang Lingxiang Research Group!

We are a dynamic, warm and friendly scientific research family dedicated to integrating cutting-edge disciplines of chemistry, physics, biology, and optics, and conducting highly creative high-level research. The research group upholds the scientific research concept of openness, collaboration, and innovation, providing members with an outstanding academic platform and growth environment.
